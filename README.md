# Skills for people who actually write

Claude is a decent writer and a worse editor than it thinks. Left alone it reaches for the safe version of every sentence, names the theme out loud, and ends on a tidy bow. These are the skills I use to stop it doing that.

They're not prompts you paste and pray over. They're craft, written down: the moves a real editor makes, turned into instructions an agent can follow. Five of them, complete, free, MIT. No email wall, no "unlock the real version." What's here is the real version.

The full kit I run — eighty-odd skills across editing, fiction, SEO, the whole operation — is the [Skills Pack]( https://nicheofone.gumroad.com/l/skills-pack ). This repo is the part I give away because it's useful on its own.

## Install

```
npx skills@latest add NoO-Network/skills
```

Or just open any `SKILL.md`, copy it into your agent's skills folder, and go. They're plain markdown. Nothing to trust but the words.

## What's in here

- **title-generator** — Titles that filter for the right reader instead of chasing everyone. Six archetypes, subtitle rules, one pick to test.
- **hook-generator** — Opening lines that put the writer inside the experience, not narrating it from across the room.
- **dialogue-authenticity** — Catches characters being used as the author's mouthpiece and makes them talk like people.
- **scene-builder** — Builds a scene as a pressure system, seven elements, with a checklist for why a flat one is flat.
- **subtext-layer** — Finds where the writing says the quiet part out loud and makes it stop.

Writing skills, on purpose. Everyone's shipping another "10x your workflow" agent. I'd rather hand you the thing that makes the words worth reading.
